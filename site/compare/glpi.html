Custovis vs GLPI

GLPI is a long-established open-source ITSM and IT asset management suite with service desk, CMDB, asset and financial management, extended by plugins; GLPI Network is its commercial cloud and subscription offer. Custovis is lighter on asset management but combines ITIL processes with a shared-mailbox helpdesk, customer portal, AI assistance and technician dispatching.

When GLPI is the better choice

GLPI is the better choice if IT asset management, inventory, licences and financial tracking are central, or if you need its plugin ecosystem and multilingual interface.

When Custovis fits better

Custovis fits better if customer-facing support, a fast agent workspace, AI assistance and on-site technicians matter more than detailed asset inventory. The Custovis user interface is currently available in German.
