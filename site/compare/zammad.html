Custovis vs Zammad

Zammad is a mature open-source customer support platform (AGPL-3.0) with many channels, available self-hosted or as a hosted service. Hosted plans are priced per agent and month, and AI features are billed per AI call. Custovis targets teams that also need ITIL change and problem management, a CMDB and field service, and it runs AI with your own provider keys.

When Zammad is the better choice

Zammad is the better choice if you need a proven omnichannel support desk with a large community, a multilingual interface, or a professionally hosted and supported service.

When Custovis fits better

Custovis fits better if customer service, ITIL service management and technician dispatching should live in one self-hosted system, or if you can only use PHP shared hosting. The Custovis user interface is currently available in German.
