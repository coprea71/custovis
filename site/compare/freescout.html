Custovis vs FreeScout

FreeScout is a lightweight open-source help desk (AGPL-3.0) modelled on Help Scout. Its core is free; features such as WhatsApp, knowledge base, end-user portal, time tracking or satisfaction ratings are sold as paid modules. Custovis offers a comparable shared-mailbox workflow and includes those features, plus ITIL processes, a CMDB and field service, at no cost.

When FreeScout is the better choice

FreeScout is the better choice if you only need a simple, fast shared inbox, want a mature ecosystem of modules, or need a multilingual user interface today.

When Custovis fits better

Custovis fits better if you want ITIL processes, a CMDB, technician dispatching or German e-invoicing in the same tool, without buying and maintaining individual modules. The Custovis user interface is currently available in German.
